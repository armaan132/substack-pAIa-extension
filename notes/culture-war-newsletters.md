## Definitions

Definitions: ... what should be the definitions of the classification? Can there be culture warriors in the center? ... 

My first inclination is to classify as extreme those who put themselves outside of democracy; as far those who entertain conspiracy theories, who put themselves outside of reasonable debate; ... 

Thinking about how to achieve a reasonable classification overlaps with the [[ideology-mapping]] project.
