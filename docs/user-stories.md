# User Stories

## Core User Persona
As a scientist, I want to discover and connect with other scientists writing blogs on Substack to broaden my research horizons and stay updated in my field. I also want to use Substack to learn about the big questions in science in general.

## Discovery Stories

### Credibility Assessment
As a scientist, I want to verify authors' academic credentials so that I can trust the content I'm reading.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Integration with academic databases (Google Scholar, etc.)
  - Display of publication history and citation metrics
  - Verification of institutional affiliations
  - Peer review participation history

### Search and Classification
As a scientist, I want to search for Substacks within my research field so that I can find relevant content.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Search functionality across Substack newsletters
  - Content classified into research fields using NLP
  - Ability to browse by research field/category
  - Clear display of search results with relevant metadata

### Cross-disciplinary Discovery
As a scientist, I want to explore big questions and breakthrough discoveries across different scientific fields so that I can broaden my understanding of science beyond my specialty.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Curated selection of "big picture" scientific discussions
  - Highlights of breakthrough research across disciplines
  - Easy-to-understand summaries for non-specialists
  - Tags/filters for fundamental scientific questions
  - Ability to follow cross-disciplinary themes

### Content Recommendations
As a scientist, I want to receive personalized content recommendations so that I can discover relevant research and discussions.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Personalized feed based on reading history
  - Topic modeling for content matching
  - Easy access to author contact information
  - Ability to save/bookmark interesting content

### Trend Analysis
As a scientist, I want to identify trending scientific topics so that I can stay current with emerging research discussions.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Analytics dashboard showing trending topics
  - Metrics including subscriber count, engagement rates, and posting frequency
  - Ability to filter trends by time period and field
  - Visualization of trend data

### Research Opportunity Discovery
As a scientist, I want to find open research questions and collaboration opportunities so that I can contribute to ongoing scientific discussions.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Identification of open-ended research questions in posts
  - Links to relevant datasets and papers
  - Classification of associated resources
  - Easy way to contact authors for collaboration
  - Ability to filter by research opportunity type
