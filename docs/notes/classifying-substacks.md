# Classifying Substacks

It is quite difficult to come up with a good classification of newsletters and other writings.

- The classification needs to be helpful for each individual reader. What classification does help me to decide what to read?
- The classification should be computed automatically, but also should be formed by each individual reader's preferences.
- The classification should reflect "the reality" in a reasonable way. (For example, one may want to take into account whether a writer is an academic employed by a non-profit institution of higher education.)
